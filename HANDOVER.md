## Current version: 0.9.9

Co-op for up to three players, over Firebase: the lobby, and now the run itself. One shared ward, one Patient hunting all of you, going down and getting each other up, proximity voice chat. The version stays 0.9.9 until the game is ready to go public (Nikola's call).

### Fixed: the camera spinning round on its own (0.9.9)

- Nikola's friend (often) and Nikola (now and then): turning, the camera suddenly did a full turn, and you ended up facing The Patient. Not lag: a known browser glitch. With the mouse locked, Chrome on Windows now and then reports one mouse move hundreds or thousands of pixels too big (2,860 px is a full turn at sensitivity 1), and the game turned by all of it. Gaming mice (fast polling, high DPI) set it off more.
- **Raw mouse input**: the mouse is locked with `unadjustedMovement` where the browser offers it (Chrome, Edge), which skips the Windows pointer handling the glitch comes from; elsewhere the usual lock (`lockPointer` in `Game.ts`). The mouse may feel a little different (no Windows pointer acceleration): Mouse sensitivity in Settings evens it out.
- **Impossible jumps thrown away** (`isMouseSpike` in `engine/input.ts`): one move over 350 px and six times the biggest of the last 0.1 s (20 px at least, from rest) is dropped. A real flick builds up over a few frames, so it's always kept. Checked in the game: a gentle turn 4.5°, a hard flick 114.7° (all kept), a 2,860 px glitch 0°. New checks in `tests/test-input.ts`.

### After the first real co-op test (0.9.9, sixth part)

Nikola hosted a Normal run with a friend over Firebase: no lag on the host, but the friend had snapping, lag and "net-code problems"; The Patient was nearly impossible to lose in a chase, which made lockers useless.

- **Smoother teammates and Patient on a real connection.** Everyone else (and, off the host, The Patient) was drawn a fixed 0.15 s in the past, blended between updates. Over a real connection updates often come later than that, so they stood still, then jumped. Now each game measures how late updates have been arriving and draws them just far enough back to nearly always have one to blend to (`targetDelay`: one gap between updates plus the slowest tenth, 0.15–0.5 s), easing that delay so what you see never jumps or runs backwards (`easeDelay`). Simulated (updates ten times a second, up to 0.3 s late at random): standing still in 27% of frames before, under 1% now; on a good connection it stays at 0.15 s.
- **Fixed: a rejoined teammate moved in jumps for the rest of the run** (their game's clock started again from zero, so every update after counted as old), and a late update could move someone back to where they'd been.
- **Your catch is decided on your own screen** (`catchesYou` in `world/enemyTargets.ts`). The host used to decide every catch with its own picture of you, a moment old, so you were caught while The Patient still looked a couple of metres off. Now off the host your own game catches you when The Patient you see reaches you; your life going 'caught' tells the host, which plays the grab or blow for everyone (it's at you there too). The host's own catches are as before. Checked on the stand-in: the guest caught at 0.5 and 0.7 m on their screen (thrown, then hit on the last life), the host taking it in a few frames later with The Patient at them.
- **Easier to lose in a chase** (`world/enemyAI.ts`):
  - **It doesn't know where you went any more.** Losing sight of you, it used to keep following where you really were for 1.2 s, round any corner and straight to where it could see you again. Now it runs to where it last saw you and a little on (at most 3 m) the way you were going, a guess. A turn it didn't see, a room, or a locker it didn't see you get into loses it.
  - **For 8 s after it loses you, your footsteps don't give you away** (Nikola's idea). You were still running when it gave up, and your steps (heard 30 m off) brought it straight back. Other noises still do: a thrown thing, a door, a switch, your voice with "Enemy can hear you talk". During a chase it never listened to footsteps anyway; it follows you by sight.
  - (Fuses sped it up 2% each for a moment; back to 4%, see below.)
- **Lockers mid-chase:** it still never opens lockers by itself (since 0.9.8). With the changes above, breaking its sight and then getting in works.
- **Then, after Nikola played it again ("he loses me way too easily"):**
  - **Chasing you, it sees you wherever it has a clear line to you** on your floor: any distance, dark or not, crouched or not (`chaseSees` in `world/enemyAI.ts`; the co-op target choice uses it too). Running down a hallway it used to lose you as if it couldn't see in the dark (13.5 m without your flashlight). It loses you only out of its sight: round a corner, through a door, up the stairs, in a locker it didn't see you get into. Getting into a locker in its sight mid-chase it sees, from any distance (outside a chase still only within 6 m).
  - **Its chase speed is back**: 4% per fuse again (5.0 m/s with all four against your 5.4). Not fast enough, it gets you.
  - **Proximity chat by distance alone**: walls don't muffle voices any more (full up close, fading out between 16 and 26 m). The lobby blurb says so.
- Not changed: everything still goes through Firebase (the host's Patient and everyone's position, ten times a second). If it's still bad for the friend, the next step is sending positions straight between the players (the voice chat already connects them directly).
- New checks: the smoothing on a bad connection (moving, never backwards, the delay's limits), a rejoin, late updates, catches on your own screen, the guess after losing you, the 8 s of deafness to footsteps; then its sight in a chase (18 m off in the dark, crouched: seen; round a corner or hidden: not; a locker in its sight), someone behind it in the hall staying its target, 4% per fuse.

### Tabbing out, rejoining, pings, the mimic, Low graphics (0.9.9, fifth part)

- **The run goes on while the host is tabbed out.** A tab that isn't being drawn (alt-tabbed, minimised, behind another tab) gets no frames, and The Patient used to freeze for everyone with the host's. Now a small worker ticks twenty times a second (`bgWorker`, `backgroundTick` in `Game.ts`): when no frame has come for a quarter of a second, it moves the co-op world on: the host's Patient keeps hunting and sending, everyone keeps sending where they are (standing still) and taking in the others. Back in the tab, the frame loop takes over with no jump. It goes by the missing frames, not the page's hidden flag (Claude's in-app browser stops drawing a background tab that still says it's visible). Filmed scenes (the wake-up, a throw) wait until you're back. Checked: with the host's tab in the background, its clock moved 2.95 s in 3 s and The Patient walked 3.6 m.
- **Rejoining** (`net/rejoin.ts`). A player who isn't the host and drops out of a run (a reload, the connection going) keeps their place: once the run starts, the server no longer removes them from the room when they disconnect. The tab remembers the run (sessionStorage: a reload keeps it, closing the tab doesn't), and the main menu offers **Rejoin the run · CODE**. Your game saves where you are about once a second (`run/sv/{uid}`: place, facing, lives, hurt, on your feet / down with the seconds left / out), so you come back right there, as you were; what you carried was put down where you dropped out ("Back in the run. What you carried fell where you dropped out."). Down when you dropped: still down, with your seconds. A run that's over: "That run is over." While you're away the team list says you left. Leaving on purpose (Leave the run) or the end screen forgets the run. **The host can't rejoin:** the host is the room, so a host who drops ends it for everyone, as before.
- **Pings: F** (rebindable, "Ping (co-op)"; `world/ping.ts`, `engine/pingView.ts`). Point and press: everyone sees a marker there for 6 s, through walls, the same size at any distance, with who pinged, what, and how far ("Ann · Fuse · 12 m"; yours leaves out your name), and hears two quick tones from it. The Patient in your crosshair (and in sight): a red "It's here!" with lower, harsher tones. Else the thing nearest where your view hits, within 1.3 m (an item on the floor, an electrical panel, a locker, the generator, the exit), in gold; else that spot ("Here"). Up to 45 m, one marker per player, every 0.6 s at most. Sent over `run/pg/{uid}`. On the controls card in co-op. Checked across two tabs: the guest's ping reached the host ("Guest · Here").
- **The privacy note in the lobby**, under the voice options: voices go straight between your computers, so the others in the room can see your IP address (as in any voice chat); with the mimic on, each game keeps a few of your lines in memory during the run and plays them only to this room; nothing is uploaded or kept after the run.
- **The mimic** (lobby: **It mimics your voices**, on by default, needs proximity chat; `audio/voiceClips.ts`, `MIMIC` in `state/coopRun.ts`). Every game quietly keeps short lines of what each of you says: whenever someone talks for 0.7–4 s, that stretch is kept, the last five per person, in memory only. The host decides when: the first time 60–90 s into the run, then every 45–90 s, and only while The Patient prowls (patrolling, looking round, checking a noise: never mid-chase, on the ceiling, or holding someone), in the voice of one of you who has talked. Every game then plays one of that person's lines **from where The Patient is**, wrong: lower and slower (0.84×), doubled by a second copy a hair lower and 35 ms late (not quite one voice), roughened, with a short echo, muffled through walls like its other sounds. Your friend calling from a dark corridor, or yourself.
- **Settings in three tabs** (Video, Audio, Controls; `theme-16-settings.css`) instead of two long columns, and **Graphics: High / Low** under Video. Low is for slower computers: three quarters of the pixels (at most 0.75 per screen pixel, against 1.5 on High) and no shadows. It switches at once, mid-run too. Checked: Low drew at 0.75 with shadows off, back to High at once.
- **Test build only: the mimic solo** (Settings → Audio → "Solo: it mimics you (test build)"; only in `npm run dev`, not on the live site). A solo run then uses your microphone (bottom left shows Mic, lit while you talk) and The Patient mimics you: first 15–25 s in, then every 20–40 s, while it prowls. With `?debug`, `__lastWard.debugMimic()` plays one of your lines from where it is right away.

### Fixed after Nikola played it (0.9.9, fourth part)

- **Lockers are a real escape now.** The 0.9.8 fix (no search after losing you in a chase) didn't cover the other ways it found you in a locker:
  1. **It opened the nearest locker after looking at something.** When it half-noticed you (or heard you) it walked to that spot, found nothing, and opened the nearest locker within 9 m: always the one you'd just got into. It never opens a locker by itself now (the search round there stays; `startLockerCheck` is gone, the `check` machinery is idle).
  2. **It tracked you into the locker.** For 1.2 s after losing sight of you in a chase it kept following where you really were, so it walked straight to the door of the locker you'd hidden in. It now only remembers where it last saw you (`!player.hidden` in `stepEnemy`).
  3. **"It saw you get in" counted from anywhere it could see you** (10 m in the dark, ~21 m if your flashlight was on as you stepped in), so in a chase it almost always had. Now only from up close: within 6 m, in front of it, nothing in between (`LOCKER.seenWithin`, `seesYouGetIn` in `state/hiding.ts`). Right on your heels and watching, it still drags you out; with a lead, or out of its sight, you're safe.
- **Fixed: voice chat didn't work.** The microphone is asked for when you press Ready, and when the last player is ready the 3 s countdown starts at once. A run that started while the mic prompt was still up went on without your microphone for good (two windows, two prompts: easily neither answered in time, so nobody heard anything). Now Ready waits for your answer ("Allow the microphone…"), and the run waits for the microphone rather than starting without it; a teammate's call waits in your inbox until then.
- **The stand-in (`?coop=local`) writes one tab at a time** (`exclusive` on the backing: the Web Locks API). Two windows each rewrote the whole shared record ten times a second and could overwrite each other's writes: positions came back at the next update, but a voice handshake or a picked-up fuse could vanish. (A test without it didn't lose anything in the in-app browser, so this wasn't proven to be the voice failure; it's a fix either way.) Firebase never had this.
- **You can see when voice isn't working:** "no voice" next to a teammate whose voice hasn't reached you, and "No mic" at the bottom left when chat is on but you have no microphone (or said no).
- Checked in the browser: two voice clients against each other (connected, audio both ways, about 200 packets each in 4 s), a received voice through the game's voice channel at -13.5 dB, two tabs on the stand-in while both write constantly (connected), and the whole flow in two game tabs with a test tone for each microphone: room with chat on, Ready (mic on), the run, both connected, each hearing the other at about -13 dB, "talking" in the team list. Not checked: real microphones and two computers over Firebase (the in-app browser has neither). New checks: lockers never opened, not tracked into a locker, seen only from close by, the stand-in's one-write-at-a-time (nothing lost, one claim wins).

### The co-op run (0.9.9, second part)

- **One ward for everyone.** The room's seed shuffles the tables, so every player has the same fuses, batteries and med kits in the same places. What anyone does to the ward reaches everyone (`net/runSync.ts`, under `rooms/{code}/run`): picking something up (if two of you grab the same fuse, one gets it, the other's toast says "Someone else got to it first."), dropping, throwing (everyone sees it fly and hears it land), using it up; fitting fuses, a panel's repair (one of you at a time: "Ann is working on it." for the others, and its door stands open), its bangs when a skill check goes wrong, its switch; the generator (the first one to start it counts; everyone gets the lights, the scream and The Patient's run for it); lockers (one of you per locker: "Someone is hiding in there."). Fuses found count for the whole team (the objective, and how fast it chases). Notes stay your own (each of you can read each one).
- **One Patient, run by the host** (`engine/coopRuntime.ts`, `world/enemyTargets.ts`). The host's game steps the AI for everyone and sends it ten times a second; everyone else draws it from that, 150 ms in the past so it moves smoothly between updates. Each frame the host picks who it hunts (`pickTarget`): the one it's chasing while it still sees them; else whoever it notices most (your beam on it, then being seen); with nobody in view, the nearest. It hears everyone's noises (the host takes each player's loudest noise from their updates, `louder`). It catches anyone it reaches, not just the one it's after (`catchable`), and finds whoever is in a locker it checks. It doesn't hunt anyone getting up, walking out, down, caught or out. While it holds someone it caught it stands there (the others see the grab and the throw), then vanishes to a far corner after 6 s, as solo.
- **Co-op Hard climbs the ceilings twice as often** (`HARD_CRAWL_BOOST`: twice the chance per second on patrol, half the wait after coming down; `crawlBoost` in `stepEnemy`). In the tests its first climb comes at about half the time on average.
- **Your teammates** (`engine/teammateView.ts`): the same body as yours, head and all, walking, running, strafing, crouching and limping like they are, with their name over their head (it turns green, with a dot, while they talk) and their flashlight beam (their own spotlight; two are made when the run starts and kept, since three.js rebuilds every shader when the number of lights changes). Your flashlight throws their shadow on the wall. You can't walk through each other (`PlayerController.teammates`; one you already overlap after a lag jump lets you walk out of them). You hear them where they are, muffled through walls like The Patient: footsteps (crouching quietly), injured grunts, flashlight clicks, picking things up, their scream when it throws them, the slam when they land. The team list top left says where each of them is: on their feet (· hurt), caught!, DOWN · 42 s, taken, got out, left.
- **Caught on your last life (Normal): you go down** (`catchOutcome`, `state/coopRun.ts`). Since the third part, it hits you (above) and you're down where you land: on your front, the screen reddens, and you have **one minute**. You can look round and **crawl** (WASD, 0.6 m/s) toward a friend. Your friends see a **revive marker** over you, through walls, the same size on screen at any distance: a red cross, your name and the seconds you have left.
